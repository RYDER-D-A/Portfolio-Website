    About Me

    Introduction

    I’m a Design Engineering student at Imperial College London. I was raised in a military family and moved around a lot growing up, which meant learning to adapt quickly to new environments. Outside engineering, I’ve always been very active. I grew up hiking and climbing mountains, competed in martial arts when I was younger, and now spend a lot of my time training in the gym and running, including half marathons and mountain routes. I also enjoy riding motorbikes, playing chess and reading.

    Work Ethic

    My work ethic is similar to how I approach my hobbies. I tend to solve problems through persistence and endurance, learning as I go and working well with the people around me. I have a strong interest in sustainability, but I also believe that for a sustainable solution to last, it needs to make economic sense as well.

    Kitchener Scholar

    I was awarded a Kitchener Scholarship after a competitive application and interview process. The scholarship is available to students from Armed Forces families, with applicants selected based on academic and personal achievement as well as their potential for future development. Given my own military-family background, it was a particularly meaningful achievement for me.
